Questions

Good to know.

Who is Gripply for?

Tradespeople and small businesses in the UK who run the business from their phone: plumbers, electricians, builders, cleaners, hairdressers and anyone whose work comes in by call and message.

What number do I get, and how fast?

We assign you a dedicated UK mobile number for calls, SMS and WhatsApp Business - hand it out to customers and everything routes to one place. Before Twilio can issue it, we check your ID and address. It's a one-time check, usually done the same day, but it can take up to 72 hours, and we'll let you know the moment your number is live.

Can I keep my existing number?

Gripply gives you a new business number rather than taking over your existing one, so your personal number stays private: calls and texts you make from Gripply show your Gripply number.

Does it work on iPhone and Android?

Yes. Gripply is an app for iPhone and Android. Tap to Pay works on Android phones with NFC running Android 13 or later, once card payments are set up.

How does WhatsApp Business work?

Switch WhatsApp on for your Gripply number from the app, a one-off setup through Facebook. After that, customers who message your Gripply number on WhatsApp land in the same thread as their calls and texts, searchable and tracked together. Photos, videos and voice notes come through too, and each voice note has a written transcript a tap away.

Does it work with Instagram and Facebook Messenger?

Yes. Connect your Facebook Page from the app, with the Instagram business or creator account linked to it, and the DMs people send them land in Gripply beside their calls and texts, with the same to-dos pulled out. Reply from Gripply, or from your Page inbox as you do now, and the thread shows both sides. Photos, videos and voice notes come through, and when someone who found you on Instagram gives you their number, Gripply joins the two into one contact. Meta only allows a business to reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message, so Gripply tells you when that window is closing.

Do callers know they're being recorded?

Yes. The first time someone rings your Gripply number, they hear a short notice, before your phone rings, that calls and messages with you are recorded and processed. If they message you first, Gripply replies with the notice instead, and one notice covers both. When you get in touch with someone new, Gripply reminds you to tell them yourself and can add the notice to your first text. Once you mark someone as personal, calls with them aren't recorded, apart from any voicemail they leave, and they don't get the notice.

Is it accurate at pulling out tasks?

Each appointment, requirement and callback Gripply hears goes in front of you as a to-do, and you can dismiss anything it got wrong. Only a time you and the customer both agreed goes straight in the diary, and money it hears about stays a suggestion until you confirm it. The missed-call text-back and the voicemail reply can each be switched off, and everything Gripply sends on your behalf is right there in the thread.

How is it different from a second SIM or the WhatsApp Business app?

A second SIM or the WhatsApp Business app gives you another number to answer. Gripply puts your calls, texts, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger in one thread per customer, records and transcribes your calls, turns what you agreed into to-dos and diary bookings, and handles quotes, invoices and payments.

What does it cost?

We'll share pricing as we roll out early access. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to hear.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel whenever you like, and it takes effect at the end of your current billing period.
