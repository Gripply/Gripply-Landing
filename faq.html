Questions

Good to know.

What number do I get, and how fast?

We assign you a dedicated business number for calls, SMS and WhatsApp Business — hand it out to customers and everything routes to one place. Before Twilio can issue it, we check your ID and address. It's a one-time check, usually done the same day, but it can take up to 72 hours, and we'll let you know the moment your number is live.

How does WhatsApp Business work?

Switch WhatsApp on for your Gripply number from the app, a one-off setup through Facebook. After that, customers who message your Gripply number on WhatsApp land in the same thread as their calls and texts, searchable and tracked together. Photos, videos and voice notes come through too, and each voice note has a written transcript a tap away.

Does it work with Instagram and Facebook Messenger?

Yes. Connect your Facebook Page from the app, with the Instagram business or creator account linked to it, and the DMs people send them land in Gripply beside their calls and texts, with the same to-dos pulled out. Reply from Gripply, or from your Page inbox as you do now, and the thread shows both sides. Photos, videos and voice notes come through, and when someone who found you on Instagram gives you their number, Gripply joins the two into one contact. Meta only allows a business to reply within 24 hours of the customer's last message, so Gripply tells you when that window is closing.

Is it accurate at pulling out tasks?

Each appointment, requirement and callback Gripply hears goes in front of you as a to-do, and you can dismiss anything it got wrong. Only a time you and the customer both agreed goes straight in the diary, and money it hears about stays a suggestion until you confirm it. The missed-call text-back and the voicemail reply can each be switched off, and everything Gripply sends on your behalf is right there in the thread.
