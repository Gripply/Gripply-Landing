Questions

Good to know.

What number do I get?

Gripply assigns you a dedicated business number for calls, SMS and WhatsApp Business. You hand it out to customers and everything routes to one place.

How does WhatsApp Business work?

Gripply connects to WhatsApp Business so those chats land in the same client timeline as your calls and texts — all transcribed, searched and tracked together.

How long until my number is live?

Your business and identity are verified through Twilio — a one-time check that can take up to 72 hours. We'll let you know the moment your dedicated number is active.

Is it accurate at pulling out tasks?

Gripply flags appointments, requirements and callbacks for you to confirm — you stay in control, and nothing gets actioned without you seeing it first.
