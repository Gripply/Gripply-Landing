Questions

Good to know.

What number do I get, and how fast?

Gripply assigns you a dedicated business number for calls, SMS and WhatsApp Business — hand it out to customers and everything routes to one place. Your business is verified through Twilio first, a one-time check that can take up to 72 hours, and we'll let you know the moment your number is live.

How does WhatsApp Business work?

Gripply connects to WhatsApp Business so those chats land in the same client timeline as your calls and texts — all transcribed, searched and tracked together. Photos, videos and voice notes come through too, and voice notes are transcribed so you can read them at a glance.

Is it accurate at pulling out tasks?

Gripply flags appointments, requirements and callbacks for you to confirm before they become commitments. Automations like missed-call text-back and quote chase-ups are optional — you choose what runs on its own, and everything Gripply sends on your behalf is right there in the thread.

What does it cost?

Two plans: Starter at £60 a month with 300 call minutes and 300 texts included, or Pro at £80 a month with 500 of each. Every feature is on both plans — the only difference is the allowance. Both start with a 14-day free trial, you can cancel anytime, and if you go over your allowance calls are 12p a minute and texts 6p each.
