How it works

From ringing phone to done — in three steps.

  1. 01

    Get your Gripply number

    We check your ID and assign you a dedicated number for calls and SMS. Switch on WhatsApp Business from the app in about five minutes, and connect your Facebook Page so your Instagram and Messenger DMs land there too.

  2. 02

    Every conversation captured

    Gripply records and transcribes your calls and threads your messages — SMS, WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger — so the full history of every customer lives in one place.

  3. 03

    Nothing slips through

    Gripply turns what you agreed into to-dos, and a visit with a day and time you both agreed goes straight in your diary.

Missed-call text-back

Miss the call. Keep the job.

When you can't pick up, Gripply texts the caller back within minutes — so they wait for you instead of ringing the next number on the list. If they left a voicemail, the text picks up on what they asked about, without quoting a price or a time. Every missed call is flagged for a callback.

Captured commitments

Every promise becomes a to-do.

Appointments, quotes, requirements, callbacks — whatever you agreed on a call or in a message, Gripply pulls it out automatically and keeps it in front of you until it's handled.

Quotes & money

From quote to paid, on your phone.

Send a quote your customer can accept from a link, and Gripply nudges you when one goes quiet. When the job's done, send the invoice and get paid by bank transfer, by card from the link, or with Tap to Pay on Android.
