The full tour

Everything Gripply does.

One business number for calls, SMS and WhatsApp, with Instagram and Messenger DMs in the same thread — and everything Gripply catches, tracks and reminds you to chase.

01

Missed-call text-back

Miss the call. Keep the job.

When you can't pick up, Gripply texts the caller back within minutes — so they wait for you instead of ringing the next number on the list. If they left a voicemail, the text picks up on what they asked about, without quoting a price or a time, and you can read the voicemail too. Every missed call is flagged for a callback.

02

Captured commitments

Every promise becomes a to-do.

Appointments, quotes, requirements, callbacks — whatever you agreed on a call or in a message, Gripply pulls it out automatically and keeps it in front of you until it's handled. Agreed something on site? Type or say it when you mark the visit done, and it's caught the same way.

03

One timeline

Every channel in one thread.

Stop hunting across your call log, your texts, WhatsApp, Instagram and your Facebook Page inbox. Every customer gets one timeline, in order. Calls are recorded, transcribed and summarised, so you can read back what was agreed, and photos, videos and voice notes land in the thread too. Calls with people you mark as personal aren't recorded, apart from any voicemail they leave.

04

Smart diary

Agree a time and it's booked.

Settle on a time during the call and Gripply books the visit for you. It shows your free gaps, warns you before you double-book, knows your working days and days off, and has a text ready when a visit is coming up, moved or called off.

05

AI daily brief

Wake up knowing exactly what needs you.

Every morning Gripply goes over yesterday's calls, your open to-dos, your quotes and the money you're owed, and hands you a plain-English brief: who to ring, what's overdue and which accepted jobs still need booking in. Start your day already on top of it.

06

Quotes

Quotes they can say yes to.

Write a quote on your phone — one price or itemised, with a deposit or stage payments — and send it where you already message the customer. You'll see when they've opened it, they accept it from the link, and Gripply thanks them the moment they say yes. If one goes quiet, Gripply nudges you to chase it.

07

Getting paid

Bill it and get paid.

Turn a finished job into an invoice from the quote and any extras you noted, and send it with your bank details and a reference. Set up card payments and customers can pay by card from their link too, with the money going to your own Stripe account, less Stripe's fee. Gripply keeps a running tally of what you're still owed.

08

Tap to Pay

Paid before you pack up.

When the job's done, tap Take payment and your customer holds their contactless card or phone to the back of yours. No card reader to carry. When Stripe confirms the payment, Gripply records it against the job. Works on Android phones with NFC running Android 13 or later, once card payments are set up.

09

Jobs

All your jobs, stage by stage.

The Jobs tab lines up every job by where it's got to — to quote, quoted, to book, booked, to invoice, unpaid, paid — and shows the one thing each needs from you next.

Also in the box

  • Know who's calling

    When your Gripply number rings, see the caller's open to-dos, the latest money mentioned, your notes and what was said last time, before you answer.

  • Replies written for you

    Tap for a reply or a chase in your usual tone, drawn from the prices and dates Gripply already has. The draft only goes when you send it.

  • Voice notes, transcribed

    Voice notes play right in the thread, with a written transcript a tap away.

  • Review requests

    Ask for the review while the job's still fresh in the customer's mind.

  • Running-late texts

    If the drive means you'll miss the start time, Gripply has a text ready to tell the customer.

  • Receipts and refunds

    Send a receipt once the customer has paid, and refund all or part of a card payment from the app.
